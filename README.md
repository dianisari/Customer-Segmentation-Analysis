# Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is part of my Data Analyst Bootcamp learning journey. The project focuses on processing and analyzing customer data using Microsoft Excel to understand customer characteristics and identify potential customer priorities based on predefined criteria.

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer data and create customer segmentation based on balance and age classification. The analysis also determines customer priority by combining customer segment and age grade.

## Dataset

The dataset contains customer-related information, including:

- Customer ID (CIF)
- Customer Name
- Age
- Gender
- Date Open
- Account Number
- Product Code
- Balance
- Average Balance

## Data Processing

The analysis process includes:

1. Data Cleaning
2. Region Classification
3. Product Name Identification
4. Customer Segmentation based on Balance
5. Age Classification
6. Customer Priority Classification
7. Data Analysis using Pivot Table
8. Summary and Key Findings

## Tools & Functions

**Tools: **

- AVERAGEIF / AVERAGEIFS

## Customer Segmentation

Customers are classified into three segments based on their balance:

- Mass Market
- Optimal
- Privilege

Customers are also classified into age grades:

- Grade A
- Grade B
- Grade C
- Grade D

The combination of customer segment and age grade is used to determine customer priority:

- High
- Middle
- Low

## Key Findings

- The majority of customers are concentrated in the Mass Market segment.
- The Privilege segment contributes the highest total balance with balance 2,919,740,465, indicating a higher funding contribution from this customer group.
- Grade B represents the largest customer group, accounting for 34% of the total customer population based on age classification.
- High-priority customers are mainly concentrated in the Jawa Barat region, indicating a potential target area for marketing activities.
- The combination of customer segment and age grade identifies B customers as High Priority, representing the most potential target for marketing
